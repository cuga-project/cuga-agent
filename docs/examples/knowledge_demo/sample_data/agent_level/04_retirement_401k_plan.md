# Acme Corp 401(k) Retirement Plan Summary

**Plan Year:** 2026
**Plan Administrator:** Fidelity Investments
**Plan Contact:** retirement@acmecorp.com

## 1. Eligibility

All full-time employees are immediately eligible to contribute to the 401(k) plan on
their first day of employment. Part-time employees become eligible after completing
1,000 hours of service within a 12-month period.

## 2. Employee Contributions

Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limits via pre-tax (Traditional 401(k))
or after-tax (Roth 401(k)) payroll deduction, or any combination of the two.

**2026 IRS Contribution Limits:**
- Standard limit: $23,500 per year
- Catch-up contribution (age 50+): additional $7,500 per year
- Enhanced catch-up (ages 60–63): additional $11,250 per year

Contribution changes can be made at any time through the Fidelity NetBenefits portal.
Changes take effect at the start of the next pay period.

## 3. Employer Match

Acme Corp matches 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation, plus 50% of the next
2%. This means employees who contribute at least 5% receive a full 4% employer match.

**Example:** An employee earning $100,000 who contributes 5% ($5,000) receives a $4,000
employer match, for a total annual retirement contribution of $9,000.

Match is calculated and deposited each pay period. There is no annual true-up.

## 4. Vesting Schedule

Employee contributions are always 100% vested.

Employer match contributions follow a 3-year cliff vesting schedule:
- Less than 3 years of service: 0% vested (forfeited on termination)
- 3 or more years of service: 100% vested

Years of service are counted from the employee's hire date. A "year of service" requires
1,000 hours worked in a 12-month period.

## 5. Investment Options

The plan offers 20+ investment funds including:
- Target Date Funds (2030, 2035, 2040, 2045, 2050, 2055, 2060, 2065)
- Index funds (S&P 500, Total US Market, International, Bond)
- Actively managed funds
- Self-directed brokerage option (additional fees apply)

Default investment for auto-enrolled employees is the Target Date Fund closest to their
65th birthday.

## 6. Auto-Enrollment and Auto-Escalation

New hires are automatically enrolled at a 3% contribution rate unless they opt out within
30 days. Contribution rate automatically increases by 1% each year on January 1 until
reaching 10%, unless the employee opts out of escalation.

## 7. Loans and Hardship Withdrawals

Loans are permitted up to 50% of the vested balance or $50,000, whichever is lower.
Maximum loan term is 5 years (15 years for primary residence purchase).
Interest rate is prime + 1%. Interest is paid back to the employee's own account.

Hardship withdrawals may be available for documented financial hardship. Consult with
Fidelity for eligibility and tax implications.

## 8. Rollovers

Employees may roll over balances from previous employer retirement plans or eligible
IRAs into the Acme Corp 401(k) at any time. Contact Fidelity at 1-800-835-5097 for
rollover assistance.

## 9. Distributions

In-service distributions are generally not permitted before age 59½ except for hardship
or qualified plan loans. After separation from service, employees may leave their
balance in the plan, roll it over, or take a distribution (subject to taxes and possible
penalties).
