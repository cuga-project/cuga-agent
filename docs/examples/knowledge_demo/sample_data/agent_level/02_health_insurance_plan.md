# Acme Corp Health Insurance Plan Summary

**Plan Year:** 2026
**Carrier:** BlueShield National
**Plan Administrator:** benefits@acmecorp.com

## Plan Options

Acme Corp offers three medical plan options. Employees must elect a plan during open
enrollment (November 1–15 each year) or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

### Option A: Basic PPO

- Individual deductible: $2,500
- Family deductible: $5,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (individual): $6,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (family): $12,000
- In-network coinsurance: 20% after deductible
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 40% after deductible
- Primary care visit: $30 copay
- Specialist visit: $50 copay
- Emergency room: $250 copay (waived if admitted)
- Employee monthly premium (individual): $120
- Employee monthly premium (family): $480

### Option B: Enhanced PPO

- Individual deductible: $1,000
- Family deductible: $2,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (individual): $4,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (family): $8,000
- In-network coinsurance: 10% after deductible
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 30% after deductible
- Primary care visit: $20 copay
- Specialist visit: $40 copay
- Emergency room: $150 copay (waived if admitted)
- Employee monthly premium (individual): $220
- Employee monthly premium (family): $780

### Option C: HDHP with HSA

- Individual deductible: $3,500
- Family deductible: $7,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (individual): $7,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (family): $14,000
- In-network coinsurance: 10% after deductible
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 30% after deductible
- No copays (all services subject to deductible and coinsurance)
- Employee monthly premium (individual): $60
- Employee monthly premium (family): $260
- HSA eligible: yes

## Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in Option C (HDHP) are eligible to contribute to a Health Savings
Account. For 2026, the IRS contribution limits are:

- Individual coverage: $4,300 per year
- Family coverage: $8,550 per year
- Catch-up contribution (age 55+): additional $1,000 per year

**Acme Corp HSA employer contribution:** The company contributes $1,000 per year for
individual coverage and $2,000 per year for family coverage, deposited in two equal
installments in January and July. Employer contributions count toward the IRS limit.

HSA contributions are pre-tax and funds roll over year to year. HSA funds can be used
for qualified medical expenses tax-free.

## Prescription Drug Coverage

Included with all plans. Uses a four-tier formulary:
- Tier 1 (generic): $10 copay
- Tier 2 (preferred brand): $35 copay
- Tier 3 (non-preferred brand): $70 copay
- Tier 4 (specialty): 25% coinsurance, max $200

Mail-order pharmacy available for 90-day supplies at 2x the 30-day copay.

## Dental and Vision

Dental and vision are separate plans. Premiums are deducted pre-tax.
- Dental: $15/mo individual, $45/mo family. Annual maximum $2,000.
- Vision: $5/mo individual, $15/mo family. One eye exam and one pair of frames per year.

## Qualifying Life Events

Outside open enrollment, employees may change coverage within 30 days of:
- Marriage, divorce, or legal separation
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Death of a spouse or dependent
- Loss or gain of other coverage
- Change in employment status

Documentation is required within 30 days for all life event changes.
