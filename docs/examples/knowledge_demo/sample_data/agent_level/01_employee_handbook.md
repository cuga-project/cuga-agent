# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 7.2

## 1. Working Hours

Standard full-time employees work 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.
Outside core hours, employees are encouraged to manage their own schedule.

Remote work is permitted for all roles classified as "Remote Eligible" in the HR system.
Hybrid employees are expected to be on-site at least 2 days per week.

## 2. Compensation

Acme Corp pays salaried employees bi-weekly, on alternating Fridays.
If a payday falls on a company holiday, pay is deposited on the preceding business day.
Hourly employees submit timesheets by 11:59 PM on Sunday each week.

## 3. Code of Conduct

All employees must adhere to the following principles:
- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Report ethical concerns through the Ethics Hotline at ethics@acmecorp.com.

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited and will result in
immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 4. Leave Types Overview

Acme Corp offers the following types of paid and unpaid leave:
- Paid Time Off (PTO) — see PTO Policy document for details
- Parental Leave — 16 weeks paid for primary caregiver, 8 weeks paid for secondary caregiver
- Bereavement Leave — up to 5 paid days per immediate family member
- Jury Duty — full pay for the duration of service
- Military Leave — in accordance with USERRA

## 5. Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year:
- Mid-year review in July
- Annual review in January

Merit-based salary increases take effect on February 1st of each year.
Promotions can occur at any time with manager and HR approval.

## 6. Professional Development

Each employee receives an annual professional development budget of $2,000
for courses, conferences, books, and certifications. Unused funds do not carry over.
Submit reimbursement requests through the Expense portal within 60 days of purchase.

## 7. Remote Work Equipment

Remote Eligible employees receive a one-time $1,500 home office stipend.
Acme Corp provides a company laptop, external monitor, and ergonomic chair upon request.
Equipment must be returned within 10 business days of termination.
