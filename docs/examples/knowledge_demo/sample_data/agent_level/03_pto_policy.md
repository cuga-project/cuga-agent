# Acme Corp PTO and Time-Off Policy

**Effective:** January 1, 2026
**Policy Owner:** HR Operations

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual

All full-time employees accrue PTO on a monthly basis. PTO is a combined bucket that
covers vacation, personal days, and short illness.

Accrual rates scale with tenure:

| Tenure | Annual PTO | Monthly Accrual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 10 hours |
| 3–5 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 13.33 hours |
| 6–10 years | 25 days (200 hours) | 16.67 hours |
| 11+ years | 30 days (240 hours) | 20 hours |

PTO accrues on the last day of each month. New hires begin accruing PTO on their first
day of employment but cannot use PTO during the first 90 days.

## 2. PTO Usage

PTO must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance for any request of 3 or more days.
Same-day requests are allowed for up to 2 days (typically used as sick time).
All requests are submitted through the Workday portal and require manager approval.

## 3. PTO Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 40 hours (5 days) of unused PTO into the next calendar
year. Any hours above 40 are forfeited on December 31. Employees are encouraged to use
PTO throughout the year to avoid forfeiture.

Exception: If an employee has a scheduled vacation in January that was blocked by
business need in December, they may request a one-time carryover waiver through HR.

## 4. PTO Payout on Termination

Upon voluntary termination with at least 2 weeks notice, unused PTO (up to the 40-hour
carryover cap) is paid out in the final paycheck at the employee's current base rate.
PTO payout is not provided in cases of termination for cause.

## 5. Unpaid Time Off

After PTO balance is exhausted, employees may request unpaid leave with manager and HR
approval. Unpaid leave does not accrue PTO and may affect health insurance premium
contributions. Unpaid leave of 30+ days must be coordinated with HR to maintain benefits.

## 6. Holidays (2026)

Acme Corp observes 11 paid company holidays:

- New Year's Day — January 1
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day — January 19
- Presidents Day — February 16
- Memorial Day — May 25
- Juneteenth — June 19
- Independence Day — July 3 (observed)
- Labor Day — September 7
- Thanksgiving — November 26
- Day after Thanksgiving — November 27
- Christmas Eve — December 24
- Christmas Day — December 25

Holidays do not count against PTO. If a holiday falls on a weekend, it is observed on
the nearest weekday.

## 7. Sick Leave

PTO covers short-term illness. For illnesses lasting 5 or more consecutive business days,
employees should apply for Short-Term Disability through the benefits portal.
A doctor's note is required for any absence of 3 or more consecutive days.

## 8. Bereavement and Other Leaves

See the Employee Handbook for details on Bereavement Leave (5 days per immediate family
member), Jury Duty, and Military Leave.
